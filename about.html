What is this about?

The Big League Tournament is a fully-realized, multiple day racing league where teams compete to gain points by racing cars that they have worked to build over the course of the tournament.

Racers will recieve a vehicle that they must customize to their maximum potential to compete in a series of races against other teams. Teams will receive points based on placements, with the top teams competing in the final round.
